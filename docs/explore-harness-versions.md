# Explorer harness versions

Every run records `harness` in its `record.json` (and `config.json`): the version below,
the git commit and whether the working tree had uncommitted changes, the sha256 of the
explorer prompt, and the explorer and confirmer models. Bump `HARNESS_VERSION`
(`cotral_maintenance/explorer/__init__.py`) on any change that can change what a run does.

Runs from before versioning (2026-11-07 to 2026-11-09 15:50) have no `harness` field; the
versions they belong to are given by date below, and can be told apart by their record:
no `budget` = 0.1; `budget` without `unit` = 0.2; `budget.unit = "images"` without `tiles` =
0.3–0.5 (by date).

| 0.7 | 2026-11-09 | The harness steers the choices. | 0.9 | 2026-11-09 | Opening: two wide photos converging on the start, from panoramas about 15 m before and after it (a sign edge on in one is face on in the other). Corners: bends of 30°+ are found; automatic bookkeeping and `mark_clear` never cross a corner; the state lists corners not yet seen close, by likelihood. Budget 16 + 2 Static images. | 0.10 | 2026-11-09 | The harness enforces what the model skipped. Every close view (zoom, look_from) is judged by the confirmer automatically: a stop becomes a confirmed candidate; a look-alike is closed; anything that "could be a stop from another angle" (new checklist item: a shelter from behind, a panel edge on, a bench, half hidden) is looked at from another panorama and judged again before it can be dismissed. | 0.12.0 | 2026-11-19 | Fixes from reviewing the first server runs (Q2 GGUF and NVFP4, f4156 and f8520). **Confirmer:** a plate alone no longer confirms (NVFP4 accepted a "96" house-number plate under a private-road sign at a dirt track); new look-alikes `prohibition_disc`, `private_plate`, `narrow_track` (coaches never stop on narrow tracks); new item `text_readable`: the Cotral design confirms unless the text is readable and is neither FERMATA nor the logo (the real f4156 sign was answered "no" on text and logo while "too far to read"); the Cotral logo with FERMATA or the design wins over a road sign in the same view; "unsure" when too small, "no" only when clearly absent; the design may look grey or washed out in poor light. **Second views** frame the first view's sight line from 0.6 to 1.6 times the estimated distance, and can confirm but never reject (the f4156 second view, aimed 10 m short, showed a bench and rejected the real stop). **confirm** only on a close view the confirmer has not judged (16 re-asks on judged zooms spent every confirmation in f8520). **The state no longer lists BEST NEXT SWEEPS**, nor does the prompt say to take them (NVFP4 followed them with no zoom, past the sign); the stall nudge no longer suggests anything. **The search ends by itself** when nothing more can be confirmed (confirmations used, or no image or tile for a close view and nothing left to confirm): with every turn a forced tool call the model could not stop, and spent up to 40 turns on free calls.
